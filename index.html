01 About

A strategic partner
for considered brands.

Executive and freelance consultant with 20+ years in fashion and textiles, and two years in e-commerce beauty. I work alongside boards and management teams to sharpen strategy and build the structures that make it real — driving profitable growth without compromising creative integrity.

My edge is strategic clarity grounded in operational reality. I know how decisions at the top translate into daily execution, and I design strategy accordingly. I connect functions, align teams, and turn complexity into clear direction — leading with empathy, communicating with impact, and building engagement that lasts beyond the project.

I combine strategic clarity with creative intuition and a strong sustainability perspective to drive profitable growth and long-term brand value — with a growing passion for how 3D and AI can elevate efficiency and decision-making.

03 Philosophy

The strongest brands are built at the meeting point of creativity, clarity and commercial intention.

Great design is not enough — it needs structure, insight and a clear narrative to become meaningful, scalable and profitable.

01

Creative vision needs strategic discipline

A powerful idea only becomes a powerful result when it’s translated into clear decisions, governance and focus.

02

Simplicity cuts through noise

I help teams reduce complexity to what truly matters — enabling speed, clarity and better decision-making.

03

Sustainability is leadership

Long-term brand value comes from responsible choices. I see sustainability as a strategic, not operational, lever.

04

Technology is an enabler

People create the result
